# Serving a meal

A shokunin is a master of his/her craft.

On the machine where you want your setup replicated,
clone your Oma-repository.
Make sure you have the Wakiita plugin installed.
Then type `/`, select Wakiita's **Shokunin** skill,
and tell your agent the following:

```text
Help me serve a meal.
```

It will ask you where your Oma-repository lives
and which parts you want to set up,
including a machine profile if applicable.

It will explain what needs installing
and any steps needing your involvement,
then carry out your requested setup in
dependency order and verify the result.
